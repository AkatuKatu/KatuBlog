# Catri
Catri is a darkner based on a well-loved GBA that's originally Asriel's, that got their battery lid lost and that's been abandoned for a while now, gathering dust on a corner. She can display different faces depending on the cartridge she has in, but the cat-like faces are her default ones.
## Personality
She's very sweet and shy, but its not hard to make her open up to new people because she's also quite approachable, just not the one to usually take the first step when getting to know new people. 
Basically, think of a shy but affectionate cat and that's her.
She's passionate about videogames, but also has a lot of cat-like mannerisms, with an easy-going personality and a profound love for naps and good company. She takes waiting and not having any attention quite well though, as a handheld she's only played sparingly and is used to being left alone.

# Storylines

## Arcade!Catri (Her lore with Kayden)
This version of Catri got lost somewhere in hometown one day that Asriel took it with him to play outside. Years later Berdly finds the console, and seeing how dusty and dirty it was he brings it home to clean it up , keeping it once he sees it works and not taking her outside in case he looses it just like the previous owner, who he assumes he have it up for lost.

That's when she's integrated into Kayden's team. He's at first skittish of her, jealous of her capacity to be taken outside by the lightner to play games, but when he sees she's kept at home with him he ends up recognising her abilities as a great playtester and rapidly becomes his right hand. However she sometimes gets on Kayden's nerves as she works much more calmly than what he'd like, while Catri respects him as a boss but works only on her own terms, so they get along but aren't really close friends.

## Main
She's somewhere lost within TV World, in a section that's out of Tenna's reach so she can't really reach other darkners there, but because she believes Asriel will sometime come back to search for her she isn't too discouraged about being lost. She's glad she's found by the fun gang as Susie ends up kind of "adopting" her, which really means that Kris lends the handheld console to her.

## Mantle Game
Tenna quickly recognizes her use at working at games, both partaking as an NPC as to help build any section in them, though as she's mostly working behind the scenes her presence is easily overlooked, however she does end up being a good friend of Ramb because of that same reason. 

Ramb, in order to hide the original Mantle Game from Tenna, trusts on Catri's to take good care of it. She's not explained the importance of the game, and curiosity ends up getting the best of her and she tries to run it herself, an opportunity that friend takes to explore around in her body.
