## Cino
From *succino*, one of the names for amber in spanish. I chose that because of its usual warm yellow hues, but also because it conserves ancient life, as the transformation uncovers Kayden's past self *(Raylen, that's what the R stands for)*.

![Drawn reference of the design of Cino](shark_RKayden.png)

* Lemon shark: social, known for their docile approach to humans. They also prefer a stalking style of hunting.
* He may seem like he got more colder because of his default poker face but he's gotten more social, clingy even.
* He's a great listener, though paired with his more silent demeanour he might seem a little stalk-y. He's just the type to observe his surroundings a lot.
* A little shy, even untrusting with strangers, but easily bonds to whoever pays attention to him.
* Having his size no longer hindered he's not only grown back to his full height but even gotten a little bigger reaching 4m (13 ft).
* He *will* silently judge you but will also keep it to himself unless provoked. 

### Design notes
* The modifications are based on external controller modifications, they wrap around and integrate into his body being a similar hard plastic-like material.
* ![Photos of controller modifications](Controllers.png "Image references used for Cino's design.")
* He's expressionless by default. When he does show his mouth a couple fangs are *always* seen.
