R. KAYDEN

Concept

More than a “gameshow host” he’s a mix between a butler and the owner of an arcade, still a host but not as flashy. He has the responsibility of taking care of the “arcade machines” (which are the representations of many videogames) and he does so as a supervisor that has many employees that do the maintenance (he’s very involved in the inspection tho).

Lightworld

Kayden is a not-so-old and rather small TV that Berdly owns for retro gaming, but in the last years he’s been getting less and less attention since Berdly’s playing more on PC. Luckily for him, the town’s internet connection being down has put him back in the spotlight, and has to take the opportunity to avoid getting put aside once again.

Personality

His main objective and wish is to entertain the lightners. He’s also stuck to the past with nostalgia and is very weary of new technology.

While working

He love his job, specially when it comes to entertaining the lightners, he takes great pride on doing a great job, but at the same time he takes it too seriously and gets really angry the second something goes wrong, specially with his employees.

The first impression he gives off with lightners is that he is very helpful and professional, but also joyful, making dumb jokes and gigs here and there ensuring everyone’s having fun. He kind of has the humour of original Tenna but is much less frenetic. However he can be quite overbearing, as inside the videogames he puts for the lightners he’s always somewhere nearby, taking the role of a neutral NPC in shops, as a guide or even in the audience withing the game.

With his workers he’s a very strict boss, he doesn’t exploit them, but his obsession with perfection is often annoying, he hates any kind of error that makes a game not “perfect” in his eyes (except if a lightner enjoys it, in which case he relaxes about it a bit). Its the kind of boss that if he doesn’t like how you’re doing something prefers to do it himself, and in turn his employees barely have any work left to.

Under professional front he’s very sensitive, and overall he’s good at hiding that, but he gets envyous really easily, making up for it inflating his ego.

Outside of work

All that joy and cordiality is replaced by tiredness, but he’s still very prideful. He’s incapable of fully disconnecting from his job at any given time.

As he hides his frustration to keep a professional front during work, outside of it he’s has no patience left and has his true feelings much more exposed, being more rageful.

Relationships

Once you’ve earned his favour (specially if you’re not a worker of his or are a lightner), he’s very keen on helping out, but he’s otherwise a bit standoffish, having to really earn his trust to get him to open up and hang out. His main way of showing affection if through acts of service and kind words, but he really likes receiving physical affection, even though he (barely) tries to avoid it during work to stay professional.

His envy also applies to interpersonal relationships, getting easily angered if he feels left out and being quite jealous in romantic relationships.

Abilities

His main special ability is that he can put anyone and himself inside any videogame (8 or 16 bit) in any moment, but he only takes the role of a neutral NPC (in theory he could take any role, but doesn’t like to). He also can change his own and the environment’s sprites at will, just so he fits perfectly in any game.

When he doesn’t want to let fo of the lightners he locks them inside a videogame, but if he gets too angry he gets glitchy and breaks the games he’s in.
